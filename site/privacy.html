Privacy

WebeSign is built to know as little as possible. There are no trackers on this site and the product needs no account.

What stays on your computer

Your certificates, your PIN and the keys never leave your computer. The app asks the operating system or the token driver to sign.

What a website receives

Only what you approve: the public certificate you chose and the signature of the hash it sent. Never your PIN or your private key.

Permissions

A website or program is remembered only if you tick the box. The list is in the app, with a Revoke button.

Logs

Diagnostic logs contain no names, ID numbers, serial numbers, digests, PINs or certificate contents. Copying diagnostics is your choice.

This website

It has no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. The language you pick is remembered only in your browser.

Contact

Questions or reports: open an issue in the source repository.
