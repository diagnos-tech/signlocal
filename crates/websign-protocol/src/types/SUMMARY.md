# crates/websign-protocol/src/types

- `algorithms.rs` — Algorithm names, spelled as WebCrypto spells them.
- `app_info.rs` — What the app says about itself in `hello` and `status`.
- `bytes.rs` — Binary data on the wire.
- `certificate.rs` — A certificate as callers receive it.
- `context.rs` — Who is asking: facts the transport attaches to requests.
- `mod.rs` — Value types that messages are made of.
