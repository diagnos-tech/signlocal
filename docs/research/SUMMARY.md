# research

Sourced research that feeds the risk proofs and the app's built-in lists; every claim carries its basis.

- `native-messaging.md` — where each browser looks for the native messaging host, launch arguments, limits, Snap/Flatpak portal, Safari, extension pre-registration
- `pkcs11-modules.md` — how PKCS#11 modules are found (p11-kit registration, known vendor paths) and the sourced path list per product and OS
- `tokens.md` — tokens, cards, and readers used in Brazil, Portugal, Spain, and Latin America (USB IDs, ATRs, middleware); seed for `devices.json`
